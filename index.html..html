They are the listed starting package prices. Confirm the exact service, availability, and final total in Discord before making any payment.

No. Never send passwords, two-factor authentication codes, recovery codes, or other sensitive account credentials.

Timing depends on the requested work and current availability. Ask for an estimate before paying; no fixed delivery time is promised here.

What if I want something not listed?

Contact the Discord server with your request. Any custom quote should specify deliverables, total price, timing, and limitations before payment.
